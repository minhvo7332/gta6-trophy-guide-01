    Roadmap to the platinum — 3 phases

    Do it in this order. Phase 1 is the story (60–80 h). Phase 2 is the open world after the credits (≈ 100 h, most of the platinum). Phase 3 is the money-and-property cleanup (≈ 10 h), ending with the 100% checklist and the platinum. Click a phase to see only its trophies.

    Missable trophies: 1 (possibly)

    Story missions cannot be replayed, so use the game's manual save slots before big missions and before Chapter 1 ends. Every trophy card's guide now has its own missable check — exactly when it can be missed and how to avoid it.
